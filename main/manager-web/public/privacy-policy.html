Introduction

Welcome to the Xiaozhi Backend Service (hereinafter referred to as "the Service"). The Service is operated by the entity that actually deploys and manages it (hereinafter referred to as the "Operator" or "we"). We fully understand the importance of your personal information and will do our utmost to protect its security.

Before using the Service, please read and fully understand the entire content of this Privacy Policy. Once you begin using the Service, you are deemed to have read and agreed to this Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy applies to our collection, storage, use, sharing, and protection of your personal information when you use the Service through the Console (admin panel), API interfaces, and other means.

I. Information We Collect

To provide the Service to you, we may need to collect the following information:

1.1 Account registration information: When you register an account, we collect your phone number or username, password, and other information to create and verify your account.

1.2 Device information: When you bind a hardware device, we collect device identification information (such as device code, MAC address, etc.), device model, firmware version, etc., for device management and service provision.

1.3 Agent configuration information: When you create and configure an agent, we collect information such as the role templates you set, language model selections, and voice parameter configurations, to provide personalized AI interaction services.

1.4 Voice interaction data: When you use the voice interaction feature, the Service uses voice activity detection (VAD) to determine the start and end of your speech, and processes your voice input data, transmitting it to third-party automatic speech recognition (ASR) and language model (LLM) services for processing, in order to enable the voice interaction feature.

1.5 Image data: When you use the vision model feature, we may process image data captured through your device's camera and transmit it to third-party vision model services for analysis and understanding, to enable features such as image recognition and scene understanding.

1.6 Conversation memory data: When you enable the memory feature, the Service stores summaries of your interaction history with agents, to provide a more coherent and personalized interaction experience in subsequent conversations.

1.7 Knowledge base data: When you use the knowledge base feature, we collect and store the documents, text, and other knowledge base content you upload, for the agent to perform knowledge retrieval and Q&A during conversations.

1.8 Voiceprint data: When you use the voiceprint recognition feature, we collect and store voiceprint feature samples of yours, for speaker identity verification and personalized services.

1.9 Chat history data: We store your conversation history records with agents, including text conversation content, conversation time, interaction context, and other information, to provide a continuous conversation experience and historical query features.

1.10 Conversation audio data: When you use the voice interaction feature, we may store audio data from your interactions with agents, to optimize voice interaction quality and retrospective queries.

1.11 Agent configuration data: When you configure an agent, we collect information such as the tags, plugin configurations, and context provider settings you set, to provide agent customization services.

1.12 Device firmware data: When you use the OTA upgrade feature, we record device firmware versions, upgrade history, and other information, for device management and firmware traceability.

1.13 Log information: When you use the Service, we automatically collect service log information, including but not limited to access time, IP addresses, browser types, and operation records, for service operations and security assurance.

1.14 Verification code information: When you log in with your phone number, we send a verification code to you via an SMS service, for identity verification.

II. How We Use the Collected Information

The information we collect will be used for the following purposes:

(1) Providing, maintaining, and improving the Service, including core features such as account management, device management, and agent configuration.

(2) Processing your voice interaction requests, identifying voice input through voice activity detection (VAD), and invoking third-party AI model services to complete speech recognition, intent recognition, semantic understanding, and speech synthesis.

(3) Processing your image data, invoking third-party vision model services to complete image recognition and scene understanding.

(4) Storing and managing your conversation memory data, in order to provide a more coherent service experience in subsequent interactions.

(5) Storing and retrieving the knowledge base content you upload, in order for the agent to provide you with more accurate knowledge Q&A during conversations.

(6) Ensuring the security and stability of the Service, including identity verification, security protection, and troubleshooting.

(7) Complying with applicable laws, regulations, and regulatory requirements.

We will not use your personal information for other purposes unrelated to the purposes described above. If your information needs to be used for other purposes not specified in this Privacy Policy, we will obtain your consent in advance.

We will select service providers with reasonable security capabilities, but please be aware that third-party service providers will process the relevant data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

3.2 Legal requirements: We may need to disclose your personal information in accordance with applicable laws and regulations, legal procedures, or requirements from government authorities.

3.3 Security assurance: We may use or disclose personal information within a reasonably necessary scope to protect the rights, property, or safety of the Operator, other users, or the public from harm.

3.4 With your consent: We may share your personal information with third parties with your explicit consent.

(1)Self-deployment: If you deploy the Service yourself, the Operator is only the code provider, and the actual data storage and processing are your own responsibility. In this case, you are both a user of the Service and the manager and protector of the data.

If you do not agree to the transfer of data abroad, you may choose to use only AI services provided by domestic service providers (such as Zhipu AI, Alibaba Cloud, Baidu ERNIE, iFlytek, etc.) or local deployment solutions (such as FunASR, FishSpeech, Ollama, etc.).

VII. Description of Third-Party Services

7.1 When providing intelligent interaction features, the Service needs to call third-party services, including but not limited to:

(1) Voice activity detection service (VAD): Detects the start and end of voice input.

(2) Automatic speech recognition service (ASR): Converts your voice input into text.

(3) Large language model service (LLM): Performs semantic understanding, intent recognition, and reply generation on text.

(4) Speech synthesis service (TTS): Converts text replies into voice output.

(5) Vision model service: Recognizes and understands images.

(6) SMS verification code service: Used for identity verification during user registration and login.

(7) MQTT message broker service (MQTT Broker): Used for message communication between devices and the server, including issuing device control commands and reporting device status.

(8) Database services: Used for persistent storage of user data, device data, agent configuration, chat history, and other information.

7.2 The aforementioned third-party service providers will process data in accordance with their respective privacy policies.
