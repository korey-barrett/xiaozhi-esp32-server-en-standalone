IV. Service Content

4.1 Service overview: The Service relies on artificial intelligence technology, invoking third-party generative AI models through API interfaces, to provide intelligent interaction services for connected hardware devices, including but not limited to voice activity detection (VAD), speech recognition (ASR), semantic understanding (LLM), speech synthesis (TTS), vision understanding (VLLM), intent recognition (Intent), conversation memory (Memory), and knowledge base (RAG).

4.2 Agent management: You can create and configure agents through the Console, including setting role templates, selecting language models, configuring voice parameters, setting intent recognition rules, managing the knowledge base, enabling conversation memory, and configuring agent plugins. The content set for agents must not violate national laws and regulations.

4.3 Device management: You can bind and manage your hardware devices through the Console, and perform operations such as device configuration, firmware updates, and OTA remote upgrades. You should ensure that the devices you manage are lawfully owned by you or that you have been authorized to manage them.

4.4 Vision understanding: The Service supports capturing images through the device's camera and invoking third-party vision models for image recognition and scene understanding. You should ensure that your image capture activities comply with relevant laws and regulations and do not infringe on the privacy of others.

4.5 Intent recognition: The Service can understand the intent of user commands through the intent recognition feature and trigger corresponding operations or services accordingly, such as controlling smart devices and querying information.

4.6 Conversation memory: The Service can record and store summaries of your interaction history with agents, in order to provide a more coherent and personalized interaction experience in subsequent conversations. You can manage or clear memory data through the Console.

4.7 Knowledge base: The Service supports you in uploading documents, text, and other content to build a knowledge base. Agents can retrieve content from the knowledge base during conversations to provide Q&A services to you. You should ensure that the knowledge base content you upload does not infringe on third-party intellectual property rights and does not contain content that violates laws and regulations.

4.8 Voiceprint recognition: The Service supports the voiceprint recognition feature. You can register voiceprint samples to enable speaker identity verification and personalized services. Voiceprint data will be used for identity verification purposes.

4.9 Voice clone: The Service supports the voice clone feature. You can clone a custom voice/timbre by providing audio samples. The cloned voice is for your personal use only and must not be used to impersonate others or to engage in illegal or improper activities.

4.10 MCP access point: The Service supports the MCP (Model Context Protocol) protocol and can act as an MCP Server to provide tool-calling capabilities to external systems, or act as an MCP Client to call external MCP services, enabling standardized integration with external systems.

4.11 Context provider: The Service supports the Context Provider feature, which can retrieve real-time information from external data sources, such as weather, news, and stocks, to provide agents with a richer source of knowledge.

4.12 MQTT protocol: The Service supports the MQTT (Message Queuing Telemetry Transport) protocol, used for message communication with IoT devices. Through the MQTT protocol, you can implement functions such as issuing device control commands and monitoring device status.

4.13 Service statement: The conversation and reply content generated by the third-party AI models that the Service relies on is for reference only and does not constitute any professional advice. You must not use the output content as professional advice in fields such as medicine, law, or finance. You bear full responsibility for any judgment or decision you make based on the output content.

4.14 Service fees: The Service is a free open source project and does not charge users any usage fees. It only provides the platform capability to invoke third-party services and does not involve any paid features or value-added services.

The Service supports multiple AI service providers, and the billing models of the different providers are as follows:

(1)Free services: Some providers offer free quotas or fully free services, such as Zhipu AI (including free models such as glm-4-flash) and Microsoft EdgeTTS speech synthesis.

(2)Pay-per-use services: Some providers bill based on API call volume, such as OpenAI, Alibaba Cloud Intelligent Speech, Baidu ERNIE, and iFlytek. You are required to pay the corresponding fees to the third-party service providers yourself; such fees are unrelated to the Service.

You can choose the appropriate service provider based on your actual needs and budget. For specific fee standards, please refer to the official documentation of each service provider.
